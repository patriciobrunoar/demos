How It Began

10 years of adventure.

A 2012 camping trip to Croatia was all it took — Femke saw a neighbor pop up a hardshell rooftop tent in seconds and couldn't stop thinking about it. She left a career in advertising to chase the idea, and Femkes Rooftop Tents was born in 2014.

The brand switched manufacturing partners in 2017 to sharpen quality, rode a COVID-era surge in outdoor demand through 2020, and is now expanding the range with new aluminium Khosi models and the all-new Lifun — with fully-equipped tent packages planned from 2026.
