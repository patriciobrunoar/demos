### About femkes (/about-femkes)
- Intro poem-style copy, then: "Let me start by introducing myself. My name is Femke..."
- Body (paraphrased): Dutch outdoors enthusiast; four hardshell tent lines (Miles Starling, Hopper, JoyCee, Khosi) plus newer Alu-Khosi and Khosi Family variants, developed with manufacturers and customer feedback since 2014.
- "How it all started" (paraphrased): 2012 Hvar/Croatia trip, neighbors' fast-folding rooftop tent inspired the idea.
- "Philosophy" (paraphrased): ambition toward a fully sustainable tent; ABS shells as recyclable-vs-fibreglass alternative; grew from small storage space to distribution shipping to 17+ European dealers, site in 3 languages.
- "De tent" (paraphrased): first prototype imported/tested from China in 2014, iterative improvements to zippers/ventilation/mosquito netting from customer feedback.
- Reviews: long-form translated review attributed to Instagram "4x4noordDAK" (paraphrased) — multi-week JoyCee test across Scandinavia; praised setup speed, included mattress, opaque fabric, no condensation, wind/rain performance, lockable gas struts, aerodynamics; ends with explicit recommendation.
- Other links/captions (mostly Dutch): Petegonecamping.com (winter-camping post), "Dutch Adventure Seeker" blog by Marion van Zutphen, "Kampeerbelevenis" event by "Bureau Peen" (Heerhugowaard), "Kampeer, Fiets en wandelbeurs" trade fair, "Vrijbuiter" dealer (Zaandam), "Dachzelt Nomaden Treffen 2017" meetup (102 cars/180 people, dachzeltnomaden.com), "LIV Outdoor" event, blog reference to writer Laura de Leeuw.
