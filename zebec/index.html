About Zebec

Engineered on water,
built for life.

Zebec is the recreational marine brand of Woosung I.B. Co., Ltd., a South Korean manufacturer positioned as the biggest boat manufacturer in the world, producing quality inflatable boats at the best value.

From kayaks and SUP boards to rigid inflatable boats, water park equipment and industrial inflatables, Zebec designs for recreational paddlers, anglers, dealers and water park operators alike — backed by dedicated in-house R&D.

Headquartered in Incheon, South Korea
