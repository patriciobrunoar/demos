About Us

Technical components engineered to last.

Scalabros was founded in 2008 by twin brothers Giovanni and Benedetto Scalabrini, initially connecting European component manufacturers with bus, train and special-vehicle builders worldwide.

In 2012 the company pivoted from trading to manufacturing, launching the Spartacos brand for sun blinds and emergency roof hatches — growing roughly 20% a year through 2020 on in-house R&D and newly-earned ISO 9001 certification, and winning the Bologna Chamber of Commerce’s Research & Innovation Award that same year.

During the 2020 pandemic, part of production was temporarily converted to surgical masks and protective gowns. By 2021 the company had returned to its core lines, opened its U.S. subsidiary Scalabros North America Inc., and launched a digitalized urban-farming initiative built on circular-economy principles.

In 2023 the range expanded again with pleated blinds and mosquito screens for RVs. Today, more than 25 people across design, technical and production roles keep every stage — from material research to finished part — inside Italy.
