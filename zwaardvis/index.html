About Us

A family business, built on precision engineering.

For more than 40 years, Zwaardvis has been producing high quality seat and table solutions for marine leisure and recreational vehicles. What started as a family workshop is now a specialist manufacturer trusted by the motorhome, caravan, and leisure boating industries worldwide.

Most of our range is still made in-house: our own metal production on modern machines, powder-coating, painting, and seat foaming with our own moulding facilities. That control over every step is what lets us guarantee fit, finish, and durability on every pedestal we ship.

Zwaardvis is part of Lande Marine — founded together with Pörtner Seats in 2010, after more than three decades of collaboration producing chairs, pedestals and table columns for the maritime leisure and commercial markets.
