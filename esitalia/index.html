About Us

Industrial systems processing Italy.

ESI Italia operates continuously in the automotive world since 1976, the year in which the distribution of spare parts for commercial and industrial vehicles took shape — evolving over time with the expansion of the product range and the adoption of new technologies, electing production and design as the cornerstones of the company philosophy.

1991 is the year the brand TOP DRIVE SYSTEM was launched, designed for the air suspension line for commercial and industrial vehicles, which has since become the most extensive catalogue in Europe with product versions for the most diverse variants and uses.

Attention to detail and the desire to satisfy an ever-widening public has enabled us to consolidate a reliable brand that is recognised in Italy and abroad.

Company engine: ideas. Innovation and the desire to adopt new technologies have enabled us to stay abreast of the times and constantly renew ourselves, while maintaining design and production Italian at 100%, relying on a network of specialised partners in the country.
