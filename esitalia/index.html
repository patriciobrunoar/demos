About ESI Italia

Half a century of precision engineering.

Since 1976, ESI Italia has been at the forefront of component design for industrial and commercial vehicles. What began as a specialist workshop in Turin has grown into a byword for engineering rigor — every suspension system, shock absorber and custom part is designed, tested and manufactured in-house, to a standard that keeps Europe’s heaviest fleets running smoothly.

Our ISO 9001:2008 certified process governs everything we build, from off-the-shelf components to bespoke mechanical solutions tailored to a single chassis.

  • In-house R&D & manufacturing
  • ISO 9001:2008 certified quality
  • Custom engineering on demand
  • Pan-European technical support

What We Build

Components engineered to outlast the road.

Pneumatic Suspensions

Supplementary air suspension systems engineered to reduce dynamic overloads, improve comfort and extend vehicle life across trucks, campers and industrial chassis.

Shock Absorbers & Hydraulics

Reinforced, hydro-dynamic shock absorbers built for intensive use — up to 30% more damping than original equipment, fully interchangeable.

Custom Mechanical Components

Bespoke parts and control systems designed around your fleet’s exact specification — from prototype to production run.
