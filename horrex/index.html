Our Story

Over 37 years of innovation.

Founded in 1987, Horrex built one of the first flyscreen doors made specifically for an RV. Since then the range has grown through pleated screens and blinds, a step-less pull bar, 100% blackout blinds, curved blind systems and honeycomb insulating fabric — with new US-market products added along the way.

Under the banner “Driving Change!”, Horrex now aims to launch at least three new products every year, as part of the Driving Change group alongside InnoSlide and Clike.

Our Products

Nine product families. One engineering standard.

From aluminium framed windows to marine-grade room dividers, every Horrex product is designed for the demands of life on the road.

Aluminium Framed Windows

Powder-coated aluminium frames, straight or curved (R5000), with double-glazed UV-resistant tinted acrylic and multi-angle telescopic hinges — compatible with UCS blinds.

Window Blinds — UCS

The “Unlimited Creative Solutions” resizable blind: modular, fast to mount, noise-free, with a 4-layer blackout fabric and fire-retardant mesh options.

Flyscreen Doors

Pleated mesh with a step-less sliding mechanism, in aluminium or PVC (E-Line) construction, with optional threshold and colour coverings.

Van Flyscreen Doors

Curved cassette flyscreens for campervan side doors, fitted to the Ducato, Boxer, Jumper, Movano, Crafter, TGE, T5/T6 and Sprinter platforms.

Rear Screens

Rear-door insect screens for vans — Ducato, Boxer, Jumper and Movano — supplied with full installation manuals and datasheets.

Ramp Screens

Purpose-built for the US “Toy Hauler” market, with an optional blackout sunshade for the ramp-door opening.

Roomdividers

Compact privacy curtains on pre-bent custom tracks — offered as the pull-bar Basic version or the slimmer, click-system XS version.

TV Blinds

A tailored blackout blind for TV and sleeping areas, custom-dimensioned to fit any onboard screen.

RV Doors

Developed with Konrad Baur GmbH: a GRP or aluminium exterior door with chrome handle, FAWO 2-point lock and an integrated flyscreen.
