# HORREX.NL — Content & Design Audit

## 0. Overview
- **Platform:** Built on Wix (mesh-layout, wixui components, static.wixstatic.com media host).
- **Site title:** "Horrex | Flyscreens, blinds & windows"
- **Languages:** English (www.horrex.nl), Dutch (nl.horrex.nl), German (de.horrex.nl) via a flag-icon dropdown in the header (EN/NL/DE).
- **Business:** Horrex Horren B.V., Vlaardingen, Netherlands — manufacturer of flyscreens, blinds, windows and related components for the RV/campervan and (newer) marine markets. Part of the "Driving Change" group (with InnoSlide, Clike, and VanCasco+ per the Dutch/German homepage).

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY

The site uses **two distinct typefaces**, both delivered as Wix-hosted webfonts (i.e., licensed/custom fonts via Wix's font system, not Google Fonts, and not a self-hosted custom upload):

| Role | Font family (as served) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Theme/default headings (H1–H4 default) | **Lulo Clean W01 One Bold** (`lulo-clean-w01-one-bold`) | A premium/licensed display font (originally by Fenotype), available through Wix's font marketplace — not a Google Font. Used e.g. for page-title headers like "Products", "ALUMINIUM FRAMED WINDOWS". |
| Override headings on homepage hero (e.g. "Horrex", "Over 37 years of Innovation!") | **Montserrat Bold** (served as `wfont_...` / labeled `orig_montserrat_bold` internally) | Montserrat is a Google Font; Wix serves it from its own CDN under a hashed filename. |
| Body copy / paragraphs / nav links | **Montserrat Light** (`orig_montserrat_light`) | Same Google Font family, light weight, used site-wide for paragraph text and nav labels. |
| Buttons | Mixed — some buttons use `helvetica-w01-light`/`helvetica-w02-light` (system Helvetica fallback), others inherit the Montserrat body font | Not a single unified button font. |

**Measured sizes/weights (computed styles):**
- H1 "Horrex" (hero): 56px, weight 400 (visually bold via font itself), white, line-height ~78px
- Page-title H3 pattern (e.g. "Products", "ALUMINIUM FRAMED WINDOWS"): 45px, Lulo Clean Bold, rendered in the yellow-green accent color over photo hero banners
- H2-level section heading ("Over 37 years of Innovation!"): 34px, Montserrat Bold, black
- "Our products" section heading: 23px, Montserrat Bold
- Nav links: 14px, Montserrat Light, line-height 29px, white on the dark navy bar, **no uppercase transform, no letter-spacing** (i.e., nav labels are shown in normal sentence case, not the all-caps style common on many corporate sites)
- Body paragraphs: 15–17px, Montserrat Light, weight 400, black, normal line-height
- Buttons: ~14–16px, weight 400, white text

No custom letter-spacing or text-transform was found anywhere I inspected (headings, nav, buttons) — all computed as `normal`/`none`. Some interior page titles are visually uppercase, but that's literal uppercase text in the CMS, not a CSS `text-transform`.

## 6. UI / BUTTON STYLES

Two distinct button styles were identified:

1. **Hero/primary CTA style** (e.g., "About us" button on homepage hero): teal fill rgba(13,102,110,0.7), 2px solid teal border, **asymmetric border-radius 2px 2px 8px 8px** (flat top corners, rounded bottom corners — a distinctive half-rounded "tab" look), white text, no box-shadow.
2. **Secondary/pill CTA style** (e.g., "Find your nearest dealer" on product pages, "Back to top" in footer): dark-navy fill (rgb(12,35,64) or teal at 40% opacity for "Back to top"), **fully pill-shaped border-radius: 50px**, white text, no shadow, 2px teal border on the "Back to top" variant.

Both button types declare `transition: all` in their computed styles.
